Cancellations

How to cancel a booking, and what to expect.

How to cancel

Go to My bookings while logged in. Any upcoming, confirmed reservation shows a Cancel button — past bookings and stays that have already checked in can't be cancelled from there.

What happens next

Once cancelled, a booking's status changes to cancelled and the room becomes available again for other guests over those dates. This is immediate and can't be undone from your dashboard, so double-check the booking before confirming.

Refunds

This is a demo booking platform — no real payments are processed, so there's nothing to refund. In a production setting, refund timing and any cancellation fees would depend on the individual hotel's policy.

Need help with a cancellation?

If something doesn't look right with a booking, reach out via Contact us.
