Privacy notice

What we collect, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), and a securely hashed version of your password — never the password itself. When you book a room, we store the booking details needed to fulfill it: dates, guest count, and the room and hotel involved.

How we use it

Your information is used to operate your account, process bookings, send account-related emails (email verification, password resets, booking confirmations), and secure your account, including optional two-factor authentication.

How we protect it

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Sensitive tokens (password reset links) are hashed before storage. All traffic should be served over HTTPS in production, and session cookies are set HttpOnly and SameSite to reduce exposure to common web attacks.

Your choices

You can update your password, enable or disable two-factor authentication, and review or cancel your bookings at any time from your account. For any other request regarding your data, contact us.

Questions?

Reach out via Contact us.
