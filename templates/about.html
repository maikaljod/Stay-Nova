About StayNova

The story behind the platform.

StayNova is a demo hotel booking platform built with Flask, MySQL, and vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. It showcases a secure login system, hotel search, room booking, and a simple admin panel.

Guests can browse hotels, compare prices, book rooms with real-time availability checks, and leave reviews after a completed stay — all wrapped in a modern, responsive interface.
