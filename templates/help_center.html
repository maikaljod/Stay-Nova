Help center

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Booking a room

Search for a destination and dates on the Hotels page, open a hotel you like, and choose a room to book. You'll see live availability before you confirm — no payment surprises after the fact.

Managing your bookings

Every booking you make is listed under My bookings once you're logged in. From there you can review your stay details or cancel an upcoming reservation.

Account & security

You can change your password, and enable two-factor authentication from your account settings on the My bookings page. New accounts need to verify their email before their first login.
