Accessibility

Our commitment to making StayNova usable for everyone.

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to search, book, and manage their stays on StayNova, regardless of ability. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines across the site and keep improving as we grow.

What we've built in

Semantic HTML and labeled form fields for screen readers, keyboard-navigable menus and forms, visible focus states, and sufficient color contrast in both light and dark mode.

Let us know

If you run into anything on StayNova that's difficult to use with assistive technology, please tell us via Contact us — accessibility feedback helps us prioritize fixes.
