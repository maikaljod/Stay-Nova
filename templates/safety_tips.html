Safety tips

A few habits that keep your account and your stay safe.

Protect your account

Use a unique password for StayNova, and turn on two-factor authentication from your account settings — it only takes a minute and adds a strong second layer of protection against anyone who gets hold of your password.

Watch out for phishing

We'll never ask for your password by email. Verification and password-reset links are only ever sent from StayNova and always point back to our site — check the URL before entering any credentials.

Before you arrive

Confirm your check-in time and any property-specific instructions from your booking confirmation. Keep a copy of your reservation details handy for check-in.

During your stay

Familiarize yourself with the property's emergency exits and contact the front desk directly for any safety concerns. In an emergency, always contact local emergency services first.
